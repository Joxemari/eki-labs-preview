When you contact us

When you write to us through the contact form or by email, we receive your name, your email address, the profile you choose (investor, farmer or other) and your message. We use them only to answer you and to follow up on your request.

  • Legal basis: taking the steps you ask us to take before a possible agreement (Article 6.1.b GDPR) and, in all other cases, our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us (Article 6.1.f GDPR).
  • How long we keep them: while we handle your request and for [12 months] after our last contact, unless a contractual relationship begins, in which case we keep them while it lasts. After that, we keep them blocked only for as long as legal claims may arise, and then delete them.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or make automated decisions with it.

When you visit the website

Our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and protect the website against attacks and abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure (Article 6.1.f GDPR). We do not use this data to identify you or to build profiles. Cookies

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary cookies for security; because they are strictly necessary, they do not require consent (Article 22.2 LSSI-CE). You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to hola@ekilabs.com; we will answer within one month.
