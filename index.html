01 · The problem

Until now, you had to choose: food, or energy.

The status quo creates a false choice. We believe that’s the wrong trade-off.

Agriculture

Protecting agriculture is a strategic imperative.

Droughts, extreme heat and soil degradation make farming more vulnerable, while rising fuel and fertiliser costs squeeze farmers’ margins. Resilient local food systems matter for food security, for stability and for rural livelihoods.

Solar energy

Solar is growing, and it needs the same land.

Solar is one of the cheapest sources of electricity to scale, but it needs vast, flat, sun-rich land: often our best farmland. The result is land-use conflicts worldwide between farmers, solar developers and policymakers.

02 · The breakthrough

We enable dual land use by scaling an innovative technology that requires minimal space, allowing farmers to keep farming.

If you had one acre of land, would you use it to produce food or electricity?

With Eki.LabsNow, you don’t have to choose.

06 · Origins

Born between Harvard and MIT.

Backed by Harvard and MIT, we designed a racking structure (patent approved), researched optimal photovoltaic modules, and tested our technology on real cereal farmlands (wheat and barley) in Zamora, Spain. Since June 2024 our first plant has been producing, and our first commercial plants, 33 MWp, are ready to build.
