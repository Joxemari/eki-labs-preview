# Social sizes · eki.labs

Sizes as the networks published them in October 2026. They change: check the network's help pages before a campaign.

## LinkedIn

| Piece | Size | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page logo | 400 × 400 | `avatars/eki-labs-avatar-night-400px.png` | 268 × 268 at least. |
| Page cover | 1512 × 256 | `linkedin/eki-labs-linkedin-page-cover-1512x256.png` | LinkedIn trims it on narrow screens: keep text in the centre. The page logo covers the lower left. |
| Profile banner (team) | 1584 × 396 | `linkedin/eki-labs-linkedin-profile-banner-1584x396.png` | Your photo covers the lower left; the logo sits on the right. |
| Feed post | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) or 1080 × 1080 | `posts/` | 4:5 is the tallest shape the feed shows uncropped. |
| Document (carousel) | 1080 × 1350 per page | `posts/eki-labs-carousel-*` | Export all pages at the same size as one PDF. |
| Link preview | 1200 × 627 | `web/eki-labs-social-preview-1200x630.png` | LinkedIn reads it from the page. |

## Editing the templates

1. Install the three fonts in `03-type`.
2. Open the `.svg` in Figma, Illustrator or Affinity. Text is live; logos are outlines.
3. Change the copy, keep the layout. Export PNG at the size in the file name.
