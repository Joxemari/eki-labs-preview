01 · Logo

The sun over three rows.

A sun and three crop rows: soil and sun, together. Use the full logo when there is room and the symbol on its own when there is not.

The symbolThe sun over three rows: soil and sun in one field.
Clear spaceKeep the height of the sun free around the logo. Minimum width of the full logo: 96 px.
NameAlways eki.labs, in lowercase, in the typeface of the text around it. Never capitalised, never set in capitals.
02 · Signature

Logo and tagline, one block.

The tagline signs covers, closings, footers and the film. It sits one quarter of the symbol below the logo and runs exactly as wide as it.

Signature · dayCovers and closings on light grounds.
Signature · nightFilm sign-off, footers, dark closings.
Vertical · daySquare and tall formats, posters, 9:16.
Vertical · nightStories, social and the vertical film.
One blockLogo and tagline travel together, as one rectangle. Never centre the tagline under the symbol alone.
WidthThe tagline runs exactly as wide as the logo, from the edge of the sun to the end of the s.
GapOne quarter of the symbol’s diameter, the same unit as the clear space.
TypeZilla Slab 400, in the ink of the logo. The sun keeps its colour; the tagline never takes it.

04 · Logo in motion

The sun rises, the rows follow.

The mark always arrives the same way: the sun comes up behind the horizon, the rows draw from left to right and the name comes in. These are the live animations.

EntranceThe web and the start of a presentation: the horizon, the sun, the rows, the name. Once, never on a loop.
Film sign-offThe last seconds of every video: the symbol settles and the name comes in.
Sunrise · nightThe sun rises behind the horizon on dark grounds.
Sunrise · dayThe same rise on light grounds, without a glow.
Rows · nightThe three rows draw from left to right.
Rows · dayThe rows on light grounds.
LoadingThe rows light up in turn while something loads.
StatesBirth, rest and loading: the symbol reacts to what the product is doing. It never spins or bounces.
Time600 ms for the mark; 360 ms for anything that enters; 50 ms between staggered items.
CurveThe sun: fast out, slow settle, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). The rows: symmetric, cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1).
NeverBounces, loops, parallax or spinning. One thing moves at a time.
Reduced motionWith prefers-reduced-motion the logo simply appears.

06 · Colour · combinations

Pairs chosen by lightness, not by taste.

Colours that sit side by side need different lightness. Sol and yerba are almost equally light, so together they shimmer. Measured with CIELAB lightness, WCAG contrast and a colour-blindness simulation.

2 · Sol with deep partnersSol goes with noche, bosque, origen, yerba campo or tierra honda. Never next to yerba.

07 · Type

Three families, three roles.

A slab serif for what we say, a clean sans for how we explain it, a mono for the numbers.

HeadlinesZilla Slab 400, tight tracking. Weight comes from size, never from bold. Sentence case with a full stop.
TextWork Sans 400 and 500, 15–19 px on the web, 24–36 px on slides. At most 68 characters per line.
Labels and dataGeist Mono 500. Labels in capitals with tracking, figures with real minus signs: −95%.
The nameThe wordmark is drawn in Geist 600 and lives only in the logo. In text, eki.labs takes the typeface around it.
