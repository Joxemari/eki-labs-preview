01 · For farmers

A second, stable income.

Farmers keep their land, their crops and their EU CAP payments, and add a stable income from eki.labs: a «second CAP». Three income streams per hectare, and a bigger total, less exposed to weather, yields and markets.

Farmers keep their EU CAP payments.In Spain, farmland under agrivoltaics stays eligible for CAP support, such as the basic income support, as long as farming remains the main activity.
Illustrative, for dry-land cereal: crop income is the smallest stream and eki.labs income roughly doubles the CAP payments. A conventional solar lease pays about the same as eki.labs income, but ends both the crop and the CAP payments. The size of each stream depends on the crop and the project.

02 · How the model works

Three steps, one model that pays for itself.

We generate more value, and we share it with rural communities.

1

Install and generate

We install eki.labs rows on farmland and connect them to the grid, or to self-consumption and community solar models, with recurring revenue.

2

Create and share value

Producing when energy is worth the most lets us offer cheaper clean electricity and share our revenue with farmers.

3

Repay and reinvest

The model pays back the project loans, shares revenue with farmers and funds our growth: more profit than conventional solar.

Three ways to work with us.

One technology, three kinds of customer.

Self-consumption

Installations for farmers.

For farms with high energy needs, like milking or irrigation. Our production curve matches their mornings and evenings.

3 farmers already power their farms with our technology.

Community solar

Electricity for nearby villages.

Affordable, local clean energy through shared infrastructure, for more resilient rural areas.

Already done with Cañizal, a village of 421 people.

Connected to the grid

Electricity for companies.

Long-term power purchase agreements: stable revenue for us, clean and cheaper energy for corporate buyers.

The most scalable model, starting with our first 1 MW project.

FutureAI infrastructure

Faster time-to-power for AI infrastructure, on farmland.

AI needs power now, and new grid connections take years. On farmland, we bring land, a grid connection, clean power and storage together, so data centers get power sooner and the value stays with the farms around them.

Grid access.

Connection rights secured early, where the grid still has room.

Ready-to-build sites.

Clean power next door.

Our solar farms alongside, with closer hourly matching of supply and demand.

Storage on site.

Bridge power.

Less land-use conflict, rural jobs, shared water, and waste heat for greenhouses and drying.
